This blog post talks about my little tale in the Rustic land. One that I come to fear and finally appreciate to its full potential.
It feels like a big milestone I have reached in my developer skill.

Learning Rust has been, overall, one of my most enjoyable and satisfying experience I've had with programming so far. So much so that I felt the need to talk about it on my blog!
As always, it's rare when I write stuff, so it's also enjoyable to know I've got some stuff to brag about. And to brag I will.

## How it begun

Profoundly in myself, I am a DevOps at heart. I manage pretty much everything, from coding myself (in case you had not understood yet), to deploying using CI/CD and monitoring the deployed applications, I truly enjoying being at complete control of the infrastructure and making sure it works all automated, nicely, and perfectly.

Like oil on running cogs just turning nicely, smoothly. Don't you like seeing things work?
